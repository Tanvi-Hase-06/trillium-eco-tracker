📖 User Guide

  1. Input Data: Enter your monthly home energy (kWh), daily petrol usage, and e-bike commute.
  2. Analyze: Click "Compile Analytics" to view your annual carbon output broken down by source.
  3. Reduce: Toggle the Abatement Options below to see how specific lifestyle shifts lower your footprint.

🔍 Understanding Metrics

  • Grid Impact: Emissions from non-renewable residential electricity.
  • Internal Combustion: Tailpipe emissions from fossil-fuel transport.
  • EV Infrastructure: Lifecycle carbon cost of electric mobility.
